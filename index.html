About TIAER & our approach

The Texas Institute for Applied Environmental Research (TIAER) was created by the Texas Legislature in 1991 to bring rigorous, applied science to real environmental challenges — beginning with water quality in the North Bosque River watershed. For more than 30 years we've helped communities, agencies, and industry find workable solutions that protect water and public health without ignoring economic realities.

We're not on any one side — because the stake we care about is the one we all share: a healthy environment and a healthy economy.

Our role is to be an honest broker: to give people the data, analysis, and tools they need to weigh the tradeoffs and chart a path forward for their watershed.
