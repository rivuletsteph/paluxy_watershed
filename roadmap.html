What guides every version

Four principles

💧

Water quality first

TIAER's bread and butter, anchored in the long-term health of the watershed.

⚖️

Objective & shared-purpose

Not on any one side — the stake we care about is the one we all share: a healthy environment and a healthy economy.

🎯

Actionable

Help people flow toward "what can I actually do" — test my water, share my data, speak up.

🍃

Digestible

Show only what people need to draw conclusions — the "Oura ring" model, not a wall of metrics.

What we're building toward

Objectives

  • 1
    Be the trusted data hub for data-center & water questions in the Paluxy — objective, science-based, and impartial.
  • 2
    Help landowners understand their situation — water quality, the Trinity Aquifer, wells, and river flow, in plain language.
  • 3
    Make the science visual — an interactive Paluxy map, and later a cross-section and a runoff model.
  • 4
    Connect people to action — surface the data that matters, then a clear path: test, share, engage.
  • 5
    Start local, design to scale — get the Paluxy really right first, then use it as a template.

The plan

Roadmap, version by version

V1

Foundation & the Map

Get the Paluxy on the screen
Now
Goal: A credible single-page site built around an interactive Paluxy map, that shares the water-quality data almost no one knows exists — and points to action.
  • Build the first-draft Paluxy watershed HTML map (send in ~2 weeks) — layers belowK

V1 map layers

  • Watershed boundary — Paluxy only (USGS WBD; gray-out outside)
  • River + tributaries (latest NHD)
  • Data center footprints (GeoJSON via Alex Ford / Texas Tribune, credited)
  • TCEQ SWQMIS monitoring stations (data linked on click)
  • Wastewater outfalls
  • Wells (TWDB Groundwater Database Viewer)
  • Springs (from NHD if findable)
  • High-res aerial basemap (impervious cover shows)
Later / draft 2: land-use & impervious-cover rasters, more water-quality datasets.
  • Share the water-quality data story — TCEQ's routine monitoring + long historical record on this segmentS
  • Add groundwater quality data — wells & the TWDB Groundwater Database Viewer◆
  • Landing page — "What's a watershed? How does this affect me?" led by water quality, in an objective voice◆
  • Action pathways — the 3-step path (document baseline → track permit → engage), surfaced clearlyS
  • Starter organizations list — Protect the Paluxy, Brazos Watershed Alliance, Public Citizen, GEAA, Texas Data Center RebellionS
  • Verify all data & contacts; keep source metadata for every layerS
V2

Understand the Science

Make the invisible visible
Next
Goal: Turn the harder water science into clear visuals — and model what added development could do.
  • Aquifer cross-section "side view" — basic version first (ArcGIS/3D), then improve; likely a separate view◆
  • Trinity ↔ Paluxy explainer — how the aquifer relates to river flowS
  • Surface-water context — where the water is flowing nowS
  • SWAT-inspired runoff visual — HAWQS / Model My Watershed: "now vs. with data centers" for stream flow, surface runoff, subsurface flow (lean on Shubo)◆
  • Impervious-cover / land-use layer — added to the mapK
